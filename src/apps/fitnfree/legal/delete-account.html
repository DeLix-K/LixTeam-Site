This page explains how to permanently delete your LixTeam FitNFree account and the data associated with it — whether or not you still have the app installed.

Deleting your account is immediate and permanent. It cannot be undone, and there is no grace period or recovery window. It does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play — cancel that separately (see below).

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FitNFree and sign in
  2. Go to the More tab → Profile
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section
  4. Tap Delete My Account, then confirm

Your account and data are deleted immediately — no waiting period, no confirmation email required.

Option 2 — By email (if you can't access the app)

Email teamlix6@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my FitNFree account." We'll process the request within a reasonable time and confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your entire user record and — because every table in FitNFree's database is linked back to your account with a cascading delete — everything tied to it, including:

  • Your profile, email, and login credentials
  • Workout plans, logs, streaks, and challenge participation
  • Nutrition logs and AI Coach conversation history
  • Sleep, mood, and habit tracking data
  • Saved exercises and connected-device data (e.g. Oura)
  • Course enrollments, digital product purchases, and trainer order records
  • Any messages, form-check videos, or voice notes exchanged with a trainer, and any reports or blocks you made

This happens at the database level the moment your account is deleted — there's no separate cleanup step and nothing is kept "just in case."

What's retained, and why

Payments are handled by Stripe, Apple, and Google, which keep their own transaction records independently of our database, for their own legal, tax, and fraud-prevention obligations — this is standard for any payment platform and outside our control. We don't retain a separate copy of your payment or order data ourselves after your account is deleted.

Cancel a store subscription

Deleting your account does not stop a subscription that you bought through Apple or Google. To stop being billed: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions; on Android, open Google Play, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
