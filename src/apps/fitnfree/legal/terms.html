The AI Coach is not a doctor, therapist, dietitian, or licensed personal trainer. It does not provide medical, mental health, or professional fitness advice, and it is not a substitute for consulting a qualified professional. Always consult a healthcare provider before starting a new exercise or nutrition program, and seek immediate professional help if you are in a mental health crisis.

AI-generated estimates (calories, macros, form feedback, readiness scores) are approximations based on the data available and may not be fully accurate. You use this information at your own discretion.

The AI features work by sending what you ask the AI Coach, photos you submit for scans, and relevant information from your profile and logs to our AI provider, Anthropic, which processes it to produce replies. FitNFree asks for your permission in the app before doing this for the first time. You can turn it off at any time in Profile under "AI data sharing", and the AI features stop working until you turn it back on.
