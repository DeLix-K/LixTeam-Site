LixTeam ("we," "us," "our") built FitNFree (the "App"), an AI-powered fitness, nutrition, sleep, and wellness companion. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use FitNFree, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By using FitNFree, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

FitNFree deals with health-related data. We only collect what the App's features actually need to function, we never sell your data, and most of your data (workouts, sleep, mood, habits) is stored privately and is readable only by you, enforced at the database level, not just in the app's UI.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (the password is stored securely hashed by our authentication provider — we never see or store it in plain text). You may optionally add a display name.

Health & fitness data

To provide the App's core features, we store the data you generate while using them: logged workouts, sets and duration, workout plans, streaks and challenge participation, habit check-ins, mood check-ins and journal notes, sleep logs (entered manually or synced from Oura, if you connect it), body weight/profile stats used to calculate calorie and protein targets, and your history of AI Coach conversations and AI feature usage (exercise explanations, nutrition lookups, scan results).

Photos and camera

If you use equipment scan, food photo scan, barcode scan, or AI form check, the App accesses your camera or photo library only when you actively choose to use those features. Photos you submit for scans are sent to our AI provider (see "Third-party service providers" below) to generate a result and are recorded in your in-app scan history; we do not otherwise store or repurpose your photos. Barcode scans send only the barcode number to look up the product.

Messages and media shared with trainers

If you message a trainer, or send them a form-check video or voice note, we store those messages and files so that you and that trainer can read and hear them. They are visible to you and that trainer, and to us where needed to handle a report or to keep the service safe. If you report or block another user, we record that report or block so we can review it and so the block takes effect.

Microphone / voice

If you use voice input with the AI Coach, speech recognition happens using your device's on-device capabilities. We do not store audio recordings — only the resulting transcribed text is sent as your message.

AI features: your permission

Before FitNFree sends anything to our AI provider (Anthropic) for the first time, it asks for your permission in the app. If you say no, nothing is sent and the AI features stay off. If you say yes, what you ask the AI Coach, photos you submit for scans, and relevant profile and log information (such as goals, workouts, nutrition, sleep and mood entries) are sent to Anthropic to generate replies. You can change your choice at any time in Profile under "AI data sharing".

Reports about content

If you report a challenge, leaderboard name, squad or trainer profile, we store your report (who reported, what was reported and your reason) so our team can review it. Reports are visible only to us.

Location

If you grant location permission, we use it to support outdoor activity tracking (e.g. logging an outdoor run or walk). Location is not collected unless you grant this permission, and you can revoke it at any time in your device settings.

Payment information

The Premium subscription in the iPhone/iPad app is bought through Apple's App Store, and in the Android app through Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment; we receive only what we need to confirm your subscription (such as a transaction identifier or purchase token and its status), not your card details. On the web, and for merchandise and live trainer sessions, payments are processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number — Stripe handles payment details directly and shares with us only what's needed to fulfil and support your order (e.g. purchase status, order type, shipping address for merch).

Website waitlist sign-ups

If you join a waitlist for a possible future feature on our website (for example, a GLP-1 mode for FitNFree), we store the email address you give us, the optional answer you choose about what would help you most, which link you arrived from, and the date. We use it only to tell you about that feature, and possibly to ask you one question about it. We do not sell or share it, and we do not add you to any other mailing list. To be removed, email teamlix6@gmail.com and we will delete it. This is separate from your FitNFree account.

Wearable data (optional)

If you choose to connect an Oura Ring account, we receive sleep, readiness, and activity data from Oura's API to power the Sleep and Habits features. This only happens if you explicitly connect your account, and you can disconnect it at any time in the App.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and personalize the App's features (workout/nutrition/sleep/wellness tracking, the AI Coach, streaks and challenges, the trainer marketplace, the guides and courses library, and the merch store)
  • To process purchases and subscriptions, and to fulfil merch orders
  • To generate AI responses (coaching messages, scan results, insights) via our AI provider
  • To maintain the security and integrity of your account and our systems, including reviewing reports about content or behaviour
  • To communicate with you about your account, orders, or support requests

We do not use your health data for advertising, and FitNFree does not integrate any third-party advertising or ad-tracking SDK.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, so the App can keep working the way you expect. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from More → Profile → Danger Zone → Delete My Account in the App. This is self-service and takes effect immediately; it cannot be undone. You can also email us at teamlix6@gmail.com to request deletion instead. See Delete your account for details. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription you bought through the App Store or Google Play; cancel that in your store account settings.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the App (edit your profile, delete individual logs, or delete your account outright); for anything else, contact us at teamlix6@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

6. Children's privacy

FitNFree is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data security

Your data is protected in transit via encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our database provider. Access to your personal health data (workouts, sleep, mood, habits, AI history) is restricted at the database level so that only your own authenticated account can read it — this is enforced by row-level security policies, not just by app-side checks. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where this happens, those providers are responsible for maintaining appropriate safeguards under their own privacy and security commitments.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
