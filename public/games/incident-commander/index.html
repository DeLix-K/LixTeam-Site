Incident Commander

An incident is unfolding and the clock is running. Investigate, contain, recover, and tell the right people. Every action takes time, and the attacker does not wait.

  • Two meters matter. Threat is how far the attacker or the exposure has got. Disruption is the damage your own response does to the business.
  • Proportionate beats fast. A heavy-handed response is penalised, and so is an incomplete clean-up.
  • Choose an incident to begin. You can replay them and try a different approach.

Everything here is fictional. Your progress stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.
