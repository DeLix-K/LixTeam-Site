Social Engineering Simulator

Colleagues, IT, executives and visitors all ask for things. Some are real. Some are not. Respond, and watch how they react.

  • Choose your replies. People push back, get annoyed and change tack, and the story branches with what you say.
  • Two things matter. Company risk (keep it low) and your relationship with the person (do not be needlessly rude, because not every request is an attack).
  • Verify, then help. The skill is checking calmly through a channel you control, not refusing everything.
