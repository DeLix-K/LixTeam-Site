Incident Simulation

Work a whole incident the way an analyst does: triage the alerts, investigate the evidence, make decisions, then write the report. Your early choices change what you face later.

  • Five stages. Triage, investigate, contain, communicate, report.
  • Precision matters. Wrong triage calls, red herrings and unsupported claims in your report all cost points.
  • Be honest in the report. If your own choices caused a problem, a good report says so.
