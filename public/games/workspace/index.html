SOC Workspace

A real alert queue, real log searches and real consequences. You are the analyst on shift. There are no multiple-choice questions to get through: you investigate, you decide, and the clock keeps moving.

  • Work the queue. Open each alert, then search the logs, open the people and machines involved and follow the process tree.
  • Pin what matters. Pin the events that prove what happened to your case timeline. Pinning noise costs you.
  • Act, but proportionately. Every action takes time and the attacker is still working. Shutting down the wrong thing hurts the business.
  • File your report. Say what happened, who and what was affected, and how it began.

Everything here is fictional. Your progress stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.
