Phish or Legit?

Ten cases: emails, calls and everyday choices. Some are genuine, some are scams or risky. Decide, say how sure you are, then learn exactly what gave each one away.

  • Read the case. If there is a link, tap it to see where it really goes.
  • Say how sure you are, then choose. Honest confidence earns bonus points.
  • Three wrong answers and the round ends. Faster answers and streaks score more.

All companies and people in this game are made up. Your progress stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.
