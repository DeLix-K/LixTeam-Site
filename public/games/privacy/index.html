Your data

Privacy notice

Last updated 15 October 2026.

The short version

The games work without an account, and they send nothing about you anywhere. Your progress is kept in your own browser. The only time we receive anything from you is if you choose to join the waitlist for a possible paid plan.

What the games store on your device

  • Your rank, XP, scores, case history and settings, in your browser's local storage on this device.
  • A saved simulation, if you leave one part-way through (kept for up to 14 days).
  • None of this is sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time with the reset buttons in the games, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • The progress code and challenge codes you can copy are made on your device and only go where you send them.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist we collect:

  • your email address;
  • two optional answers: which description fits you best, and what monthly price would feel fair;
  • a short tag for the link that brought you here, if there was one (for example ?src=friend).

Why: to email you about whether a paid plan is launched, and about major updates to these games, and to learn whether such a plan is worth building. We will not charge you for anything by joining, and no payment details are collected.

Legal basis: your consent, which you give by ticking the box and joining. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it: LixTeam decides how it is used. The details are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, and this site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps ordinary technical logs such as IP addresses for a short time. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising.

How long: until you ask us to remove it, or twelve months after the demand test ends, whichever is sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop emailing you, at any time. Email teamlix6@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and tracking

The games set no cookies and use no analytics or advertising trackers.

Ranks and scores in the games are game progression. They are not qualifications, not validated assessments, and not a guarantee of job readiness.
