Your data

Privacy notice

Last updated 23 October 2026.

The short version

The games work without an account, and without one they send nothing about you anywhere. Your progress is kept in your own browser. We only receive something from you if you choose to join the waitlist for a possible paid plan, or if you choose to create a ROOTLINE account to appear on the leaderboard.

What the games store on your device

  • Your rank, XP, scores, case history and settings, in your browser's local storage on this device.
  • The days you have played (for your day streak) and your results on the weekly cases, also in local storage on this device. Resetting your practice stats clears the streak.
  • A saved simulation, if you leave one part-way through (kept for up to 14 days).
  • None of this is sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time with the reset buttons in the games, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • The progress code and challenge codes you can copy are made on your device and only go where you send them.

Accounts and the leaderboard (optional)

You can create an account to appear on the leaderboard. Nothing in this section applies unless you do. Accounts are for people aged 16 and over, and you confirm that when you sign up.

If you create an account we hold:

  • your email address (if you sign in with Google, GitHub or Apple, the email address that service shares with us, which may be a private relay address);
  • your password, if you use one. It is stored only as a salted hash by our sign-in service. We never see it and cannot read it back;
  • the nickname you choose, and whether you want to be shown on the leaderboard;
  • that you confirmed you are 16 or over, and when;
  • your best medal for each mission, and when each one improved, so we can build the weekly board and limit how fast a score can grow;
  • reports about nicknames or scores that you send, and reports other players send about you.

What other players see: your nickname, your mission XP, how many missions you have passed and how many golds you hold. Never your email address or any real name. You can hide yourself from the leaderboard at any time on the account page. Please do not use your real name as a nickname.

Scores are self-reported. They come from the progress saved in your browser. The server checks that they are possible, but cannot prove the missions were played. We may hide a player from the leaderboard, or remove results, if a nickname is offensive or a score looks fake.

Why, and the legal basis: to run the account you asked for (contract); to show your nickname and score publicly, which you choose to do and can undo (consent); and to keep the leaderboard fair and safe, including handling reports (legitimate interests).

Who handles it: LixTeam decides how it is used. Your account and results are stored with Supabase, which provides our database and sign-in service, and this site is hosted by Vercel. If you choose to sign in with Google, GitHub or Apple, that service learns that you signed in to ROOTLINE and shares your email address with us. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising.

On your device: while you are signed in, a sign-in token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It is not a cookie, and it is removed when you sign out.

How long, and deleting it: until you delete your account. On the account page you can download everything held about you, and delete your account, which removes your nickname, results, reports about you and (in the same step) your sign-in. Progress saved in your browser is not touched. Copies in our database provider's routine backups disappear as those backups expire. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we will delete it.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist we collect:

  • your email address;
  • two optional answers: which description fits you best, and what monthly price would feel fair;
  • a short tag for the link that brought you here, if there was one (for example ?src=friend).

Why: to email you about whether a paid plan is launched, and about major updates to these games, and to learn whether such a plan is worth building. We will not charge you for anything by joining, and no payment details are collected.

Legal basis: your consent, which you give by ticking the box and joining. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it: LixTeam decides how it is used. The details are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, and this site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps ordinary technical logs such as IP addresses for a short time. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising.

How long: until you ask us to remove it, or twelve months after the demand test ends, whichever is sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop emailing you, at any time. If you have an account, you can download and delete your data yourself on the account page. Email teamlix6@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and tracking

The games set no cookies and use no analytics or advertising trackers. If you sign in, a sign-in token is kept in your browser's local storage (see above).

Ranks and scores in the games are game progression. They are not qualifications, not validated assessments, and not a guarantee of job readiness.
